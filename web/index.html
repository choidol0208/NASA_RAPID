Fire Evidence Explorer

Tell us what you want to build: a spacecraft cabin, a Moon base, a rover. See which NASA microgravity fire investigations come closest, what to consider and what their recorded tests say, and where the evidence stops.

The challenge says that the low-gravity combustion data in NASA's PSI database "can be hard to find, harder to compare, and harder yet to interpret". This page takes them in that order: Find, Compare, Interpret.

Research exploration tool built on NASA Physical Sciences Informatics (PSI) data. It is not operational fire-safety guidance, and it is not endorsed by NASA.

      Fire Evidence Explorer

      Team RAPID · NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026

      Research exploration tool built on NASA Physical Sciences Informatics (PSI) data. It is not operational fire-safety guidance, and it is not endorsed by NASA.

      Scores (0–100) and order are the team's similarity computation, not a NASA result, and not operational safety guidance.

      Data: NASA Physical Sciences Informatics, 24 combustion investigations (dataset DOIs on each result; CC0-1.0 where stated in the record). Challenge: Flame in Freefall, NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026.

      Built during the hackathon with AI assistance (Claude). Catalog values are AI-extracted from the PSI records unless marked as checked. Source code: github.com/choidol0208/NASA_RAPID.
